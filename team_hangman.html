🪢 Team Hangman

Team Hangman

2–4 teams, everyone on their own device. Each round one player is the Word Master and types a secret word or phrase. Teams take turns guessing letters before the hangman is finished.

— or join one —

How to play

  1. The Word Master types a secret word or phrase (and an optional hint). Keep it school-appropriate!
  2. On your team's turn, anyone on the team can tap a letter. Right letter = +10 per letter and you go again. Wrong = a body part is drawn and the next team goes.
  3. Think you know it? Tap Solve. Right = +50 plus 10 for every letter still hidden. Wrong costs a body part.
  4. 6 misses and the hangman is done: nobody scores and the Word Master earns a 🏆 Stumper.
  5. Most points when the rounds run out wins!

The host's device runs the game, so the host should stay on this page until it ends.
