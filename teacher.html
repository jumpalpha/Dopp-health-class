🧑‍🏫 Substitute Access
Make a day-pass code so a sub can run the live games. It can't open this dashboard or see student data, and it expires on its own.
✏️ Name change requests
Students ask on their Quest page. Nothing changes until you approve. Only the first name / nickname changes; last name (used to sign in) never does.
🐣 Manage sprites
Rename or delete any student's sprite. Your rename doesn't use up the student's weekly rename. A deleted sprite is gone for good; the student hatches a new one next visit (their care history for the checklist is kept).
🔀 Duplicate accounts
Students who made a second account, usually by typing their first name as their last name. Merging combines everything into one account: XP, streak days, arcade scores (best kept), checklist, quests, trail and sprite. The student keeps the PIN and nickname they used most recently. Keep = the account whose real last name is kept.

🔑 Find a student: reset PIN, rename, move or delete
Search by first or last name. Set PIN: PINs are stored scrambled, so set a new 4-digit one and tell the student. Rename: fix a first name or last name. Changing the last name changes how they sign in (class code + last name). Move: put them in another period; progress stays and they sign in with the new class code. Delete: erases the student and all their progress for good.
👋 Visitors (past students)
Open Visitor page ▶
Past students who still play the arcade. Accounts are removed on their own 90 days after the last visit. Removing one deletes that visitor's scores and sprite. Class students are never listed here.

🌄 Wellness Trail convoys
Open the Wellness Trail ▶
Student-made teams of up to 4 wagons. Each student keeps their own journey; removing someone only takes them out of the convoy (their journey and scores stay).

What happens when you press Start:
1. This trimester's Hall of Heroes, arcade leaderboards and sprites are saved forever in Past Champions and the Sprite Hall of Fame.
2. Every sprite graduates. New students hatch new ones.
3. New class codes open (one per period), and the old codes close.
4. Every current student becomes a visitor. They keep their last name and PIN and sign in on the Visitor page. Visitor accounts are removed after 90 days with no visits.
This can't be undone.

💡 Verified XP: How Students Earn It
Verified XP comes only from work the server checks itself, so it can't be faked. Quest XP still shows, but it's self-checked.
🎓 Chapter Mastery Checks · ON
10 random vocab questions graded on the server. 8/10 passes for +50 XP, once per chapter (up to 1,400). 10-minute wait after a miss. Page: mastery.html
⚡ Live game answers · ON
+5 XP per correct answer in Vocab Showdown or Realm Raid (students must join with their Quest login).
🏆 Live game bonuses · ON
+25 XP for a Showdown top-3 finish, or for the top team when a Raid boss falls. Awarded when you press End game.
