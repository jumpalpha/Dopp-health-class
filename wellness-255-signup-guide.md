# How to Sign Up for Wellness 255 (and Get It Paid For)

**A step-by-step guide for Thunder Ridge students taking Mr. Dopp's college class**

You don't need any college experience for this — just follow the steps in order. There are two parts:

1. Register for the college class through CSI
2. Use your free state education money (Advanced Opportunities) so it doesn't cost your family anything

**Do Part 1 before Part 2.** You can't ask the state to pay for a class you're not registered for yet.

---

## Before you start

Talk to **Mr. Dopp** first and confirm you're taking **WELL 255 — Wellness Through Healthy Living** (3 college credits) through the **College of Southern Idaho (CSI)**, taught by him at Thunder Ridge High School. Ask him which section is his — sometimes more than one section shows up online, and he'll know which one is correct.

---

## Part 1: Register for the class through CSI

CSI uses a website called **DualEnroll** for high schoolers taking college classes while still in high school. This is separate from applying to CSI as a regular college student — it's built for exactly your situation.

### Step 1 — Create your DualEnroll account

1. Go to **[csi.dualenroll.com](https://csi.dualenroll.com)**
2. Click **"Create My DualEnroll Account"**
3. Fill in your name, birthdate, email, and select **Thunder Ridge High School**
4. Click **Create Account**
5. Check your email for a message from **noreply@dualenroll.com** and click the **"Confirm my account"** link inside it
6. Log back in and fill out the **Student Application** form — this is just basic info about you and your school

*Heads up: confirming your email does NOT register you for the class yet. You still have one more step.*

### Step 2 — Register for WELL 255

1. Log in to DualEnroll and click the **"Courses"** tab
2. Search for **"WELL 255"** or **"Wellness Through Healthy Living"**
3. Use the filters to find the section taught at **Thunder Ridge High School** by **Alan Dopp** (ask him if you're not sure which one)
4. Click the course title to open the **Course Detail** page
5. Under **"Available Sections,"** pick the right one
6. Click **Register**

Registration can take up to **two business days** to process — don't worry if it's not instant.

### Step 3 — What happens after you register

- You'll get an email or text confirming your enrollment
- You'll also get a **CSI Student ID number** and a **CSI student email address** — save this email, you'll need it later
- You can view your class schedule by logging into **[my.csi.edu](https://my.csi.edu)** (MyCSI)

## Part 2: Get the state to pay for it (Advanced Opportunities)

Idaho gives every public school student in grades 7–12 access to **$4,625** total (over all your years of middle and high school) to help pay for exactly this kind of thing. This program is called **Advanced Opportunities (AO)**. Dual credit classes like WELL 255 cost **$75 per credit**, and since it's a 3-credit class, that's **$225 total** — a small piece of your $4,625, and it doesn't come out of your family's pocket if you set it up right.

### Step 1 — Create your Advanced Opportunities account

1. Go to **[advancedops.sde.idaho.gov](https://advancedops.sde.idaho.gov)**
2. Click **"Create an Account"**
3. Fill in your information to set up your account

### Step 2 — Talk to your school's AO designee

Every high school has one staff member (usually a counselor) in charge of approving Advanced Opportunities funding requests locally. **Ask the Thunder Ridge counseling office who your AO designee is** — this is the one part of the process that's specific to your school, so they'll walk you through exactly how Thunder Ridge handles it.

### Step 3 — Request funding for WELL 255

With your AO designee's help, submit a funding request in the AO portal for your CSI WELL 255 class. At some schools the counselor submits this for you once you tell them you've registered; at others, you submit it yourself in the portal. Your designee will tell you which applies at Thunder Ridge.

### Step 4 — Confirm it's approved before the deadline

Follow up with your AO designee to make sure the funding request was approved **before CSI's tuition deadline for the semester** (check the [CSI Early College calendar](https://www.csi.edu/early-college/dual-credit/default.aspx) for exact dates). If it's not approved in time, the class could show up as unpaid on your account.
