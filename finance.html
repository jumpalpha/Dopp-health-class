The Cost of Your Future

Can your dream career afford your dream lifestyle?

    Step 0

    Understanding Interest

    Before you plan a life, know what borrowing money costs. Interest is the price you pay to use someone else's money.

    Simple interest for 1 year
    $70.00
    $1,000 × 7%
    Simple interest, 5 years
    interest only on what you borrowed
    Compound interest, 5 years
    interest also charged on past interest (once a year)
    Simple vs. compound: Simple interest is figured only on the amount you borrowed. Compound interest is also charged on interest that has already built up, so the cost grows faster over time — the gap between the two tiles above. Real loans and credit cards can also have fees, minimum payments, and different compounding schedules, so always read the terms.

    Check your understanding

    You borrow $2,000 at 5% simple interest for one year. How much interest do you owe?

    Research Real Living Expenses

    Now find out what life really costs. Your locked guesses are shown in blue; put what you find in the gold boxes.

    Step 5

    Taxes, Income & Affordability

    Your salary isn't what you take home. Taxes come out first. Does what's left cover your researched lifestyle?

    🧪 Try a what-if (this never changes what you turn in)

    Experiment: raise your salary, find a roommate, drive an older car… Your real numbers stay saved exactly as they are.

    Step 6

    Written Reflection

    Write at least five complete sentences in your own words. Answer each question:

    1. What surprised you most about the cost of living?
    2. Which expense did you underestimate the most?
    3. Can your chosen career support your desired lifestyle?
    4. What changes could improve your financial situation?
    5. What did you learn about financial health?
    0 of 5 complete sentences0 words

    A complete sentence has at least four words and ends with a period, question mark, or exclamation point. A quality reflection answers all five questions, uses your own numbers, and is at least 80 words. Your writing saves automatically.
