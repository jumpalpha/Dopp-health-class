Creative · Side Quest

🎵 Song Maker

Turn a health lesson into a song. Build your prompt below the way you would on Suno, hand it to your teacher, and your finished track lands in the Class Jukebox for everyone to hear.

How this works today: Suno doesn't offer a public tool for websites to generate songs automatically yet (they only opened an invite-only developer preview in mid‑2026, with no public release). So this page can't generate a song live in your browser — instead, you build the prompt here, your teacher runs it on their Suno account, and drops the finished song into the jukebox below. Same creative process as using Suno directly, just with a teacher in the loop.
