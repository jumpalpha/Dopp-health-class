Start a game (1 minute)

  1. On the classroom computer, go to
    doppsclass.com/live_host.html
  2. Pick a game from the list.
  3. Type the sub code and press Start game.
  4. Put that screen on the projector.

⚖ Pick a Side

Class vote + discussion · 10–20 min
  1. Choose a chapter and click a question.
  2. Press Start vote. Students vote on phones.
  3. Press Close voting to show the split.
  4. Let students discuss, then press Revote to see who changed their mind.
  5. Next question or End game.

⚡ Vocab Showdown

Speed quiz with scores · 10–15 min
  1. Pick the chapters, 10 questions, 20 seconds.
  2. Wait for names to appear, then press Start.
  3. Each question closes by itself when time runs out.
  4. Press Leaderboard, then Next question.
  5. The last screen shows the winners' podium.

🐉 Realm Raid

Teams vs. a boss · 15 min
  1. Pick the chapters and 3 teams.
  2. Students are put on teams automatically.
  3. Press Start. Every right answer hits the boss.
  4. After each question: Leaderboard, then Next.
  5. Defeat the boss to win!

📲 How students join

Students go to doppsclass.com/live.html (or tap Join Live Game on the home page, or scan the QR code on the screen), then type the 6-digit game code shown on the projector. For Showdown and Raid they sign in with their Quest login first, so scores are saved with their names.

🛠 If something goes wrong

  • Page froze or closed? Reopen the same page. The game comes back.
  • A student can't join? Check the 6-digit code, or have them refresh.
  • "Needs your Quest login"? The student signs in at doppsclass.com/quest.html first.
  • Code not accepted? Check spelling. Codes stop working after the last day above.

✅ Good to know

  • Students may use phones only for the game.
  • Votes in Pick a Side are anonymous on the screen.
  • Always press End game when you're done.
  • The sub code only runs games. It doesn't show grades or student records.
