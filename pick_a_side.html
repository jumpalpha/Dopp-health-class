How it works
1. Gut vote1 minute. No research, no discussion.
2. Evidence4 minutes. Read both columns, even the one you dislike.
3. Partner talk5 minutes. Compare what stood out.
4. Flip side6 minutes. Make the best case for the side you did not pick.
5. Vote again4 minutes. See who moved, and why.

Light key: Green the evidence does most of the work. Yellow people who have read the same evidence still disagree. Red a values question, opened by a teacher. Cards marked Evidence report a finding from the linked source. Cards marked Argument are reasoning that people on that side use.
