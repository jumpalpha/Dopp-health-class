❤️ CPR & AED Side Quest

When someone's heart suddenly stops, every minute without CPR lowers their chance of surviving. Hands-Only CPR is two steps: call 911 and push hard and fast in the center of the chest. Learn it here, practice the beat, and walk through an AED.

🤝 This gets you ready for hands-on practice in class (Chapter 27). It doesn't replace it: you'll practice compressions and the AED steps in person, and Mr. Dopp checks it off.

1 · Check & call ✓ Done

Someone collapses and won't wake up. Before you touch them:

⚠️Is it safe?Look for traffic, fire, water or wires. Don't become a second patient.
👋Tap & shoutTap their shoulders: "Are you OK?" No response and not breathing, or only gasping? Start.
📱Call 911 on speakerPut the phone next to you on speaker so you can push while the dispatcher coaches you.
🏃Send for the AEDPoint at one person: "You! Get the AED and come back." If you're alone, call and start pushing.
Then push hard and fast in the center of the chest until help takes over.

  • Push hard: at least 2 inches (5 cm) deep on a teen or adult.
  • Let it come back up: let the chest rise all the way between pushes (full recoil). Don't lean on it.
  • Don't stop: keep pauses short. Switch with another person about every 2 minutes if you can.

4 · Use the AED ✓ Done

An AED (automated external defibrillator) talks you through every step. First, drag the two pads onto the bare chest where they belong. The pictures on real pads show you too.
