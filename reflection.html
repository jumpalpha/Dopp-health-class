📝 Reflection Coach

Write your personal reflection paper on the 8 dimensions of health by talking it through. The coach asks one question at a time (about 36 in all), helps you dig deeper when it counts, and organizes your answers into a paper. It saves after every answer, so you can stop and pick up later.
