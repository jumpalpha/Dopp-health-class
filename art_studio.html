Art Studio

The studio draws random curvy lines that cross over each other. Every space the lines make is yours to decorate. Pick a design, pick your colors, and tap a space.

🧘 Mindful break: slow, repeated drawing like this can calm your body's stress response. It's one of the coping skills from Chapter 4.
Drawing lines…

1 Draw the lines

More crossing points = more, smaller spaces to decorate. Try 20 for a quick one, 80+ for a challenge.

2 Pick a design

3 Pick colors

Space color (background)
Design color (lines, dots, shapes)
This is what a space will look like. Tap a space on the paper to use it.

4 Decorate

Tap any space. Change the design or colors and the space you just tapped (the blinking one) updates too.

5 Save it

The blank coloring page has just the lines, ready to print and color by hand. Your drawing is saved on this device, so you can come back to it.
