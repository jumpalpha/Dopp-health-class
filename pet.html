📖 How your sprite works
🌱 1. Care for it every day Pick up to 6 of 12 healthy habits you really did today. Log 3 or more and it's a care day: +1 growth and +1 to your streak 🔥. You can also answer chapter questions to feed it.
📈 2. Egg to adult in 30 care days Your egg hatches on your first care. Then it grows by care days:
🥚 Egg🐣 Baby · day 1🌿 Child · day 8⚡ Teen · day 18👑 Adult · day 30
Care for it 30 days in a row and it's an adult in 30 days. A missed day just doesn't count.
⏳ 3. Its needs drop every hour Food, Energy, Mood and Clean go down all the time. When they get low, Health drops. About 4 days with no care and your sprite fades away. Different habits fill different needs, so mix them up.
👻 4. If it fades
  • It loses 3 care days of growth (it can shrink a stage).
  • You lose 25 Quest XP.
  • Growth and your Checklist sprite care days freeze until it's back.
  • To revive: answer 10 chapter questions correctly. It comes back at 40 health.
💡 5. Knowledge Every right answer earns 1 💡, up to 20 a day (the first 10 also feed it). Spend it on the Care Kit (each item once a day) and on Shop gear. Bigger gear unlocks after more correct answers.
✨ 6. What kind of adult? How well you cared decides its adult form: Radiant (great care), Steady, Scruffy (spotty care), or Sugar-Crashed (too many treats).
