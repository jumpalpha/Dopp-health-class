Day one: get set up

About 10 minutes. Do this on your own school device.

  1. Get your class codeMr. Dopp gives each period its own code. It's usually on the board.
  2. Sign in on the Quest pageTap "Sign in with my class code." New here? Tap to create your hero. Use your real first and last name so Mr. Dopp can match your work to the gradebook.
  3. Make a 4-digit PIN and write it downKeep it private. Your device remembers you until the trimester ends, so you won't type it every day. Forgot it? Mr. Dopp can reset it. Don't make a second account.
  4. Hatch your Wellness SpriteTap My Sprite in the top menu. It's the first item on your checklist.
  5. Open your ChecklistThe gold button at the top right of every page. It's your to-do list for the whole trimester.

Every class day

  1. Open TodayYour first visit to doppsclass.com each day opens it for you. Read Mr. Dopp's briefing, vote in the Survey of the Day, and see what's due today and soon, with how long each takes.
  2. Do what's due, in orderWork from top to bottom. Most items check themselves off when the site sees you do them (🔒 means "checks itself").
  3. Check your Checklist %The little 📋 button in the bottom-left corner shows your progress on every page. Finishing a realm's chapters on your Checklist earns its badge and XP.

How each chapter works

Chapters 1–16 are required. Do them in order; each step builds on the one before. Chapters 17–28 are a lighter version (read, Learn, Mastery) that's open from day one.

Step 1📖 Read

Open the chapter. Scroll to the end of every lesson and keep it on screen at least 15 seconds. Reading is how we learn. So read it!

Step 2✅ Quick Checks

Two quick questions after each lesson. Practice only, no grade.

Step 3🎮 Vocab games

All 4: Flashcard, Speed Quiz, Word Builder, Concept Map. 70%+ to count.

Step 4✏️ Classwork

Learn the words (80% opens the next chapter) → Chapter Test → 3 short true stories using vocab words.

Step 5🎓 Mastery

10-question practice check. Get 8 right. Retake anytime.

Step 6📝 Schoology

Do the chapter's Schoology homework, then tick "Homework done" on your checklist.

End of each unit: take the Unit Test in Classwork (80% to pass, retakes allowed) and the unit test in class when Mr. Dopp gives it. After Chapter 9 comes the Roots & Branches family project.

All trimester long

These are on your checklist too. Chip away at them, a little at a time.

  • Wellness Sprite: each day pick up to 6 of 12 healthy habits you really did (3+ = a care day). 30 care days grows it from egg to adult. If it fades it loses growth and 25 XP. Your checklist wants 10 care days and 30 chapter questions right.
  • Wellness Trail: play on 3 different days.
  • Live class games: join at least 3 when Mr. Dopp runs one (Join Live Game).
  • Arcade: play every solo game at least once, and each partner game with a classmate.
  • Weak spots: replay a vocab game you scored under 80% on, or retake a Mastery check you missed (twice).
  • CPR/AED: the hands-on part happens in class; Mr. Dopp checks it off.

Grades and turning things in

  • Work you do on doppsclass.com saves itself. There's nothing extra to turn in for it.
  • Files (drawings, screenshots, certificates, videos) go to the matching assignment in Schoology.
  • Schoology is the gradebook. Your Checklist % counts toward your grade, so keep it climbing.
  • Boxes that say "Tick when done" are on your honor. Only tick them when it's really done.

Tips for a strong trimester

  • Sign in on your own device. Never sign in as someone else or share your PIN.
  • Don't skip ahead in games or click through readings. The site counts real work, not clicks.
  • "AI Guide" activities open ChatGPT, an outside site. Never type your name or personal information there.
  • Fall behind? Open Today, then your Checklist, and do the oldest thing first.
  • Lost? Tap the ❓ Help button in the corner of any page.
