Pass = every box ✓. Hand position: heel of hand on the center of the chest, lower half of the breastbone, other hand on top, arms straight. Rate on the 110/min metronome (side quest → Class practice). Depth: hand reaches the 2-inch line on the ruler beside the trainer. Recoil: back up to the top line each time. Pauses: under 10 seconds, including while pads go on. AED in order: power on → pads on bare chest (upper right, lower left side) → plug in → stand clear while analyzing → "shock advised": all clear, press shock → resume compressions right away.
